# Multimodal Co-Attention Transformer for Survival Prediction in Gigapixel Whole Slide Images

## Why it matters

MCAT is one of the **first widely-cited multimodal pathology AI architectures**, from Mahmood Lab. Where prior multimodal pathology methods used hand-crafted feature concatenation or late fusion, MCAT introduces **cross-modal co-attention Transformer fusion**: each modality (WSI features and genomics features) attends to the other through learned co-attention layers, producing a joint representation directly used for survival prediction.

MCAT is the immediate predecessor of {{ node_link("porpoise-2022") }} in Mahmood Lab's multimodal survival line and the canonical reference for cross-modal Transformer fusion in pathology.

## Core idea

```
WSI patch bag         bulk genomics (RNA-seq / mutation / CNV)
       ↓                          ↓
CLAM-style MIL              MLP encoder
       ↓                          ↓
histology features         genomics features
       ↓                          ↓
         co-attention Transformer
       ↓                          ↓
joint representation
       ↓
Cox survival head → hazard score
```

Co-attention layers compute attention from histology to genomics and vice versa, producing a fused representation that captures cross-modal interactions.

## Inputs and outputs

- **Input**: WSI patch bag + bulk genomics vector.
- **Output**: continuous hazard score.

## Datasets / tasks / metrics

- **Cohorts**: TCGA — multiple cancer types with paired WSI + genomics.
- **Tasks**: survival prediction, multimodal fusion.
- **Metrics**: C-index, hazard ratio.

## Method

- **Histology branch**: {{ node_link("clam") }}-style MIL on patch features.
- **Genomics branch**: MLP on RNA-seq / mutation / CNV vectors.
- **Fusion**: co-attention Transformer (cross-modal attention).
- **Loss**: Cox proportional-hazards.

## Main results

MCAT outperforms histology-only MIL + Cox baselines and genomics-only Cox baselines across multiple TCGA cancer types. Establishes co-attention as a strong multimodal fusion architecture for pathology.

## Limitations

- **Requires paired multimodal data** — limits cohorts.
- **Co-attention Transformer** adds parameters vs simple concatenation fusion.
- **Default ResNet encoder** is now superseded by pathology PFMs.
