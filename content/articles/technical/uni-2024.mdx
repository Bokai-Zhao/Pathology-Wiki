# UNI — Towards a General-Purpose Foundation Model for Computational Pathology

<ArticleCard id="uni-2024" />

<LocalGraph nodeId="uni-2024" hops={1} />

## Why it matters

Before UNI, pathology pipelines mostly relied on ImageNet-pretrained CNNs or a handful of pathology-specific encoders (CTransPath, Phikon, Lunit-DINO). UNI is one of the first pathology foundation models that explicitly targets **general-purpose** patch-level representations across many tissue types, evaluated on a broad downstream suite (33 tasks at slide and patch level). Together with GigaPath, Virchow, H-Optimus, and CONCH, it anchors the modern PFM cohort that any new method should benchmark against.

## Core idea

Train a ViT-L/16 with the **DINOv2** self-supervised recipe on **Mass-100K**, an internal corpus of ~100k H&E whole-slide images (~100M patches) spanning ~20 tissue types. Use the resulting frozen encoder as a drop-in feature extractor for downstream classification, MIL aggregation (ABMIL/CLAM/TransMIL), and retrieval.

## Inputs and outputs

- **Input**: H&E patches (commonly 256×256 or 224×224 at ~20× / 0.5 μm/px).
- **Output**: per-patch embedding (ViT-L hidden dim — typically 1024).

## Method

- **Architecture**: ViT-L/16 (~307M parameters).
- **Objective**: DINOv2 (self-distillation with masked image modelling).
- **Pretraining data**: Mass-100K (~100k WSIs, ~100M patches, internal — not publicly redistributable).
- **Compute**: large-scale GPU pretraining (paper reports detailed setup).
- **Downstream evaluation**: 33 tasks across patch-level classification, slide-level MIL, and retrieval, compared against ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50 / ViT, CTransPath, Phikon, Lunit-DINO.

## Datasets / tasks / metrics

- **Pretraining**: Mass-100K (internal).
- **Eval datasets** (selected): 33-task evaluation benchmark covering many public datasets — see paper for the full list.
- **Tasks**: tissue-type classification, tumor-vs-normal, subtype classification, grading, biomarker prediction, slide-level survival proxies.
- **Metrics**: AUC, balanced accuracy, top-k accuracy.

## Main results

UNI generally outperforms ImageNet baselines and prior pathology PFMs (CTransPath, Phikon, Lunit-DINO) on the majority of downstream tasks under frozen-feature evaluation, with consistent gains on rare-tissue and few-shot settings. See the paper for per-task numbers — this page does not reproduce metrics to avoid drift.

## Limitations

- **Pretraining data not redistributable** — Mass-100K is internal, blocking from-scratch reproduction.
- **Patch-level only** in the primary release — slide-level objectives require downstream MIL or pooling (consider GigaPath for slide-level pretraining).
- **H&E only** — IHC/IF tasks need a different encoder.
- **Gated weights** on Hugging Face — pipelines requiring fully open weights should consider CTransPath or open ViT alternatives.
